03 · Research

Research

Augmented reality for making hard numbers easier to understand, from my graduate coursework at FGCU.

Published · IEEE ICIICE 2026 Co-author

Augmented Reality for Corporate Sustainability Reporting: An Interactive Carbon Footprint System

Anuvinda Balan, Lee Bernard & Md Baharul Islam. Proc. 2026 International Conference on Integrated Intelligence and Cognitive Engineering (ICIICE), Dubai, 18–19 April 2026, pp. 1–6. IEEE. doi:10.1109/ICIICE69672.2026.11565311 ↗

Sustainability reports are dense PDFs full of numbers nobody can picture. We built an Android AR app in Unity and AR Foundation that recognises a company logo and floats three scaled spheres above it for Scope 1, 2 and 3 greenhouse gas emissions, using Herc Rentals' 2022–2024 Corporate Citizenship Report data. Tapping a sphere opens category breakdowns and EPA equivalencies (trees planted, EV charges, diesel burned) so a figure like 358,841 MT CO2e actually means something.
